## 2025-03-05 - Accessible Close Buttons with Textual Icons
**Learning:** In this application's dark theme, close buttons heavily rely on textual icons like `[X]` or `[ESC]`. A screen reader might try to read these out, which is unhelpful without an `aria-label`. Furthermore, just giving them `aria-label` isn't enough; the decorative icons should have `aria-hidden="true"`. Because of the dark theme and custom elements, they must also receive explicit `focus-visible` ring styles to ensure they are visible when navigated to via keyboard.
**Action:** When updating icon-only buttons, specifically wrap textual icons in `<span aria-hidden="true">` to prevent screen readers from reading characters like brackets or x's, while also ensuring `aria-label` is on the parent button and explicit `focus-visible` styles are added.
